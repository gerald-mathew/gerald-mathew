## About

I'm a 3rd-year B.Tech Software Engineering student at the Federal University of Technology Owerri, graduating in 2029. My focus is full-stack and systems work: platforms that hold real user data, authentication that has to be right, and backends that stay readable as they grow.

Most of what I've built so far has been for a real requirement rather than a demo — a tutoring centre that needed enrolment and instructor assignment tracking, a hospital that needed appointment and patient management, and a school needing a results portal, an exam platform and a site of its own.

I'm open to internships, freelance projects, and collaboration.
