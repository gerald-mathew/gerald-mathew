## About

I'm a 3rd-year B.Tech Software Engineering student at the Federal University of Technology Owerri, graduating in 2029. I work mostly on full-stack and systems projects: platforms that hold real user data, authentication that has to be right, and backends that stay readable as they grow.

Most of it has been for an actual requirement rather than a demo. A tutoring centre needed enrolment and instructor-assignment tracking. A clinic needed appointment and patient management. A school needed a results portal, a computer-based testing platform, and a website of its own.

I write C++ for command-line work and TypeScript for the web side. I'm based in Owerri, Nigeria.
